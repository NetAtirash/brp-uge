# BRP UGE v1.21.5 — Character Creation Quality Update

- Fixed vs EDU-scaled occupation budget configurable in world settings, independent of EDU display.
- New Actors and NPCs receive baseline Skill items from installed source packs/world items (when available); no hardcoded rulebook content.
- Wizard now saves all baseline skills, not just allocated/occupation skills. Deduplication prevents repeat imports.
- Wizard filters powers by enabled family; preview rules before and after adding.
- Starting age roll is player action; only GM may override current age. Age preview recalculates after change.
- Occupation selection, powers and skills show original English names alongside Thai translations.
- Reworked selected power and Personality displays into consistent rows with details.
- Reduced fixed rulebook wording in UI labels and improved tooltip/title behavior.
- Normalized HTML-formatted rulebook notes for readable display.
- Tightened contrast for Light/Dark wizard, skill rows, reference panels, notes and form controls.

- Re-entering the Wizard preserves untouched skills and avoids unintended allocation resets.
- NPC sheets show original English names alongside localized Thai skill and power labels.
