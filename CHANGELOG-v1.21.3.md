# BRP UGE v1.21.3 — Natural Paper & Contrast

- Uses natural paper generated by image generation, processed as a material asset for Light and Dark appearance.
- Removed old algorithmically generated noise textures.
- Full HD paper backgrounds are scaled to cover and never tiled.
- Strengthened secondary, caption, hint, placeholder and explanatory text contrast in the Light and Dark appearances.
- Preserved the optional Genre/Theme module separation and existing BRP engine logic.
